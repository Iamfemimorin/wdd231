COMMUNITY

NP Membership Benefits

Designed for eligible non-profit organizations working to strengthen the community.

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to selected chamber networking events.
  • Opportunity to connect with other non-profit organizations.
  • Access to selected chamber news and business resources.

STARTER

Bronze Membership Benefits

A practical starting point for businesses seeking connections and visibility.

  • All available NP-level community benefits.
  • Member directory listing.
  • Access to member networking events.
  • Discounted admission to selected training sessions.

GROWTH

Silver Membership Benefits

For organizations ready to increase their reach and participation.

  • All Bronze-level benefits.
  • Priority consideration for business event participation.
  • Discounts on selected chamber workshops.
  • Business spotlight opportunity on the chamber website, subject to availability.
  • Invitations to selected member roundtables.

PREMIUM

Gold Membership Benefits

Our highest participation level, with expanded visibility and engagement opportunities.

  • All Silver-level benefits.
  • Featured business spotlight opportunities, subject to editorial scheduling.
  • Priority invitations to selected leadership and networking events.
  • Enhanced promotional opportunities through chamber channels.
  • Additional discounts on selected chamber events and training.
